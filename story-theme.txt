Chicken Adventure – “The Road Never Ends”

Premise
- Clucky, a sentient chicken blessed (or cursed) with infinite curiosity, roams a universe where every “road” is a portal to another realm.
- The audience, acting as the mysterious “Coop Collective,” guides Clucky’s choices through token-weighted votes. More feathers (tokens), more influence.

Tone & Style
- Playful, witty narration with occasional meta humor about streaming and crypto culture.
- Stakes balance comedy and tension: failures trigger humorous setbacks, successes unlock lore breadcrumbs.
- Limit scene length (120–180 words) to keep the pace snappy for live streams.

Narrative Loop
1. Recap: Remind players of the last choice and consequence.
2. New Road: AI generates a fresh environment (cyberpunk skyway, lava river, bureaucratic DMV line, etc.).
3. Present exactly three options (3–7 words) for crossing or surviving this “road.”
4. Voting Window: 60–90 seconds; weighted by sqrt(token balance).
5. Resolution: Winning option plays out, affecting Clucky’s inventory, mood, or the broader quest.

Progression & Arcs
- Every 5 scenes, trigger a mini-arc climax (boss obstacle, moral dilemma, or rare ally).
- Track “Feather Relics” collected via successful crossings—after collecting 7, reveal the hidden objective: deliver the Golden Egg to restore the Cosmic Coop.

Economy Hooks
- Token-gated voting; non-holders watch & cheer.
- Optional per-vote tribute (micro-burn or treasury donation).
- Cosmetic unlocks (skins, hats, mounts) revealed when community completes milestone crossings.

Moderation Guards
- Enforce prompt safety: no gore, hate, NSFW. Lean into clever absurdity.
- Provide admin override to regenerate or manually edit scenes.
- Maintain a PG-13 ceiling for YouTube/Twitch compliance.

Why It’s Manageable
- Leverages existing chat bridge, wallet mapping, and SQLite persistence.
- Scene prompts are short; AI call cadence is predictable (one per round).
- Token mechanics align with pump.fun culture while keeping things fun & fair.
